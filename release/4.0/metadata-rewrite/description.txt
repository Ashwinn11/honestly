Your morning belongs to you.

Before the messages, the feeds and everyone else's opinions, take a moment to hear your own.

Honestly is your space to write what’s on your mind, understand how you feel and keep the words you need close. A few honest lines are enough. You don’t need a perfect routine or something profound to say.

WRITE FIRST. UNLOCK YOUR APPS.
Ever open your phone for a moment and lose the morning? With Premium, the apps you choose stay blocked until you finish your journal entry. Give yourself a little space before you start scrolling.

FIND WORDS WHEN YOUR MIND FEELS FULL.
Start with how you feel. Premium journal prompts meet you there, whether you’re having a hard morning or looking forward to the day. Tap Shuffle when you need a different starting point, or simply write what comes.

KEEP WORDS THAT FEEL LIKE YOURS.
Choose affirmations for self-worth, calm, confidence, hard days, intention or gratitude. Build a rotation you want to come back to. Premium lets you add your own words, choose more affirmations, see them in widgets and receive a reminder at your chosen time.

UNDERSTAND HOW YOU FEEL.
Some days feel heavier than others. Check in with your mood and see your days together on a calendar. Give yourself room to notice what’s changing.

SEE HOW FAR YOU’VE COME.
Keep a record of the mornings you showed up for yourself. With Premium, search your full journal history and return to the thoughts, small wins and moments you might otherwise forget.

MAKE THE PAGE YOURS.
Write a few lines or stay a little longer. Add a photo, a doodle or a small detail that helps you remember. Premium adds more ways to personalize your pages.

START WITH WHAT YOU NEED.
Journaling, mood tracking, your mood calendar, a streak and up to five affirmations in your rotation are free. Premium adds app blocking, mood-based prompts, full searchable history, unlimited affirmation picks, your own affirmations, widgets, reminders and manual iCloud backup and restore.

You don’t have to figure everything out this morning. Start with one honest line.
